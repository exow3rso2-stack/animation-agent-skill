# Ep 01 · PEN VS. ERASER · narration (EN, ~150 words, ~58 s)

He can delete anything he points at.
Anything... except the guy who draws faster than he deletes.
This is Hex. Whatever he draws becomes real.
Kip's glove needs three seconds to recharge. Hex needs one.
Spears. Kip deletes one... and rewinds past the other.
Walls. He's boxed in.
Then Hex drops a grid. Inside it, you can only move along the lines.
So Kip stops fighting the grid... and deletes the corner holding it up.
Gravity flips. Now they're fighting on the side of a skyscraper.
Hex just... draws stairs.
So Hex draws a cage. Kip deletes it. There's another cage inside. And another.
Three seconds is too slow.
So Kip does the only thing faster than drawing.
He deletes the roof.
Two hundred meters. One pen.
Hex draws a parachute. Just one.
He looks at Kip...
and holds out his hand.
Would you take it?
